Prceptual Bias 感知偏差

The emergence of various new communication media has occupied our lives, and people's bodies' perception of the things around them is gradually deviating. The work is based on the research of unconscious states. Distance experiments were conducted on 57 samples, starting from the real measured road sections, and comparative experiments were carried out. Through information collection and data visualization, it presents people's perception of the distance of paths, tells the perceptual bias in a visual way, and reminds people to pay attention to the importance of "body perception" in understanding and participating in society, culture and the environment.

​

各种新兴传播媒介的出现占据着我们的生活，人们的身体对周遭事物的感知逐渐出现偏差。作品基于无意识状态研究，对 57 个样本进行距离实验，从真实测量的路段出发，进行对比实验。通过信息收集和数据可视化，呈现出人们对待路径的距离感知，以可视化的方式来讲述感知偏差，提醒人们关注“身体感知”对理解和参与社会、文化和环境的重要性。
