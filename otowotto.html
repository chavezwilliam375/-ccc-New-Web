Otowotto

otowotto is a line of flight within seamless beauty — a rupture through which what lies concealed beneath convention and order emerges in an oblique gesture.A romantic impulse toward undoing, a restrained and delicate contingency, a state suspended between wholeness and fracture — poetic suspension held in potential, yielding in its undulation while keeping its edge concealed.

​

otowotto 是无缝之美中的裂口，让隐藏于常规与秩序后的暗涌，以畸角的姿态浮现。浪漫的破坏欲、克制而微妙的偶然、游移于完整与破碎之间的未完成，诗意悬置，在起伏里示弱藏锋。
