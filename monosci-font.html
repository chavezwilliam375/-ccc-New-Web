Monosci Font

Monosci is a Latin typeface family defined by monolinear strokes and geometric structures, comprising three styles: Round, Angular, and Angular Extended. The design explores curves, angular cuts, and horizontal proportions, creating distinct typographic rhythms through a shared stroke language.

Round uses continuous curves to shape counters and shoulders, creating fluid letterforms. Angular translates these curves into straight lines and diagonal cuts, emphasizing structural clarity. Angular Extended builds upon the same angular language while expanding the horizontal proportions of the letterforms, creating a more open and extended spatial expression.

The three styles are connected through variations in contours and proportions, establishing distinct visual rhythms within a unified design language and offering greater possibilities for headlines, short texts, and experimental typography.

​

Monosci 是一个以单线感笔画与几何结构为核心的拉丁字体家族，包含 Round、Angular 与 Angular Extended 三种样式。设计围绕曲线、切角与横向比例展开，在相近的笔画语言中形成不同的文字节奏。

Round 通过连续曲线组织字腔与肩部，形成流动的轮廓。Angular 将曲线转译为直线与斜向切角，呈现更明确的结构感。Angular Extended 延续切角语言，扩大字形的横向比例，使文字获得更舒展的空间表现。三种样式通过轮廓构成与比例变化建立联系，在统一的设计语言下形成各具特点的视觉节奏，为标题、短句及实验性排版提供更多可能。
